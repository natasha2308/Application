Monthly challenge: teach one small thingSample · one month
The idea
Everyone is invited to share one small skill in a short written post, such as a shortcut, a recipe or a trick for focus. Colleagues reply with what they tried.
Before it starts
One clear announcement on the first working day, a link to a short how-to, and a check that the theme fits one of the five company values.
During the month
A gentle reminder each week. One optional live hour, run twice at different times so people in Asia and the Americas can both come.
Keeping it fair
Joining is always optional. Anything live is also written up, so nobody misses out by being asleep.
How I'd measure it
Participation by region, one question in the next pulse survey, and a short note on what to change next month.
What I'd ask first
Does this fit the 2027 engagement plan, and is there budget for a small prize?
